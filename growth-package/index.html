Part two

The Autoblog Keeps It Working

A new website ranks for nothing on day one. Google has no reason to send anyone to it yet, and publishing is how that changes. Almost nobody does it, because writing never makes it to the top of the list.

The Autoblog System fixes that. You record a few minutes of talking about something you already know. The system transcribes it, drafts it in your voice, structures it for search, adds verified sources and an image, and puts it in a dashboard for your approval. Nothing publishes without your sign-off unless you switch on auto-publishing yourself.

Because the posts come from your own words, they show the first-hand experience that search engines, and AI tools like ChatGPT and Perplexity, look for when deciding whose pages to trust. It is the same work serving both.

A recent Google update devalued mass-produced AI content, the kind published in bulk with nothing behind it. AI content itself is not penalized. How it is used is what counts, and a post drafted from your own recording is the opposite of mass production.
