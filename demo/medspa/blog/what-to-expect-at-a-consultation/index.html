What to Expect at Your Consultation

May 20, 2026 · By the Belencia team

Featured image

Every plan at Belencia starts with a consultation. It is the best way to find out whether a treatment fits you before you commit to anything.

We ask about your goals. What bothers you, what you want to keep and how much downtime suits your life.

We review your health history. A licensed provider checks medications, allergies and anything that could affect treatment.

You get a plan in plain language. Which treatments, in what order, over what timeline and at what cost. You decide what, if anything, to book.

This is general information only. Treatment suitability is decided by your provider.
