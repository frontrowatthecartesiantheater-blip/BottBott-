Why Sunscreen Still Matters in Winter

May 6, 2026 · By the Belencia team

Featured image

Ultraviolet light reaches your skin in every season, and cloud cover does not block all of it. Daily sunscreen is one of the simplest ways to protect your skin and your treatment results.

Choose broad spectrum. Look for a product labeled broad spectrum with SPF 30 or higher that you will actually wear every day.

Make it the last step. Apply it after moisturizer and before makeup, and reapply if you are outdoors for long periods.

Ask us. If you are unsure which formula suits your skin, your provider can recommend options at your next visit.
