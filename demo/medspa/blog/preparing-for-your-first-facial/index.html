How to Prepare for Your First Clinical Facial

June 3, 2026 · By the Belencia team

Featured image

A clinical facial works best when your provider knows your skin and your goals. A little preparation makes the visit more comfortable and the results easier to build on.

Before you arrive. Skip harsh exfoliants and retinoid products for a few days unless your provider tells you otherwise. Arrive with a clean face if you can, and avoid heavy sun exposure beforehand.

Tell your provider everything. Share the products you use, any medications, allergies and recent treatments. Mention anything you are hoping to change, whether that is dullness, breakouts or fine lines.

Afterward. Follow the home care advice you are given, use sunscreen daily and avoid picking at the skin. If something feels wrong, call the clinic rather than waiting.

This is general information only. Your provider will advise you based on your skin and health history.
