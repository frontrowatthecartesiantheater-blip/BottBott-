Frequently asked questions

Yes. A consultation with a licensed provider is required before treatment, so we can review your health history and agree a plan.

Treatments are performed by licensed providers under the supervision of our medical director.

How much do treatments cost?

Pricing depends on the treatment and your plan. You will see the cost during your consultation, before you decide to book.

It varies by treatment. Your provider will explain what to expect and give you aftercare instructions.
